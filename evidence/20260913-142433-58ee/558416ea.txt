ADGM’s FSRA issues notice regarding unauthorised domains misusing Sarwa’s name
ADGM FSRA: 05 Jun 2026
Download Arabic
The Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) of ADGM would like to bring to the attention of the financial services community and members of the public the existence of a number of domains that falsely purport to be associated with Sarwa Digital Wealth (Capital) Limited (Sarwa), an entity duly authorised and regulated by the FSRA.
Domains of concern:
https://apply.sarwa.email;
https://meetify.cloud/sarwa/interview;
https://hr.sarwa.me;
https://sarwa.team;
https://sarwa.com.co; and
https://indeed.sarwa.me (together the “Domains”)
Sarwa has confirmed to the FSRA that it has no connection to, and is not responsible for, any of the Domains. The Domains appear to be impersonating Sarwa, including by falsely presenting themselves as part of an employment or recruitment process operated by Sarwa.
The FSRA would like to make it clear that the Domains are not associated with or operated by Sarwa. Members of the public, job seekers and others who encounter the Domain are advised to exercise caution and to refrain from providing personal or financial information through them. Sarwa has confirmed that its only official careers site is https://careers.sarwa.co/.
For information on how to report directly to the FSRA concerns in relation to potential fraudulent websites that purport to relate to financial services in ADGM, please refer to the FSRA’s Complaints Portal, which can be accessed here.
In addition, for more information on how to report cybercrime to the relevant authorities, please click here.
Only firms authorised by the FSRA can undertake Regulated Activities in ADGM.
To determine if an entity has been granted a Financial Services Permission by the FSRA to carry on Regulated Activities in or from ADGM, you can search the FSRA’s Public Register.
