Security and Regulations
Is Sarwa regulated?
We are regulated by Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) The Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) .
You can find us on the public registry of the FSRA here:: https://www.adgm.com/public-registers/fsra/fsf/sarwa-digital-wealth-capital-limited
In terms of applicable regulation:
Sarwa Invest is regulated by the ADGM Financial Services Regulatory Authority.
Sarwa Trade is regulated by the ADGM Financial Services Regulatory Authority.
We are a fully licensed and regulated online financial advisor and wealth manager. This is awesome because it gives you access to the most innovative solutions, while still having the peace of mind that Sarwa adheres to strict laws and very high standard of compliance of top tier regulators.
