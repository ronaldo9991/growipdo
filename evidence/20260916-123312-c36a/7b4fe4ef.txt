12. Sarwa
Description: Investing and trading platform
Headquarters: U.A.E.
Date of Establishment: 2017
Founders: Mark Chahwan, Jad Sayegh, Nadine Mezher
Sarwa allows users to trade U.S. stocks, ETFs, cryptocurrencies, and options through Sarwa Trade. It also provides hands-off investing with managed portfolios and an estimated 4.6% annual return with Sarwa Save Plus. Sarwa hit its first-ever profit milestone in Q1 2024 and has sustained it since, recently marking its first full calendar year profitability.
